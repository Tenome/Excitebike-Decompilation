Excitebike Tile Editor

Tiles (top: pattern table 0, bottom: table 1)

Tile numbers are the ones the game and the track editor use. The game reads background tiles from table 1 and sprites (riders, shadows, banners) from table 0 or 1 depending on the screen.

Colours

Pick which of the four swatches a click on the master palette sets. “Game sets” are the palettes the game used on the race screen (background 0-3, sprites 4-7).

Master palette (64 colours)

Edited master colours are saved with “Save .pal” (192 bytes, RGB) and load in the port with --palette. Tiles are saved with .chr or .bmp (--chr / --chr-bmp). In the port, F6 reloads them.
